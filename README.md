# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that transforms raw Amazon sales data into actionable business insights using **Python, MySQL, and Power BI**.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow—from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to SQL-based business reporting and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Amazon sales data to answer key business questions related to:

- Sales performance
- Product performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Seller performance
- Geographic sales distribution
- Payment methods
- Sales trends over time

The insights generated can help business stakeholders make informed decisions regarding product strategy, customer engagement, and revenue growth.

#  Key Business Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- A small number of products contributed a significant share of total sales.
- Credit Card emerged as the most frequently used payment method.
- Monthly sales showed noticeable fluctuations, highlighting periods of growth and decline.
- Customer purchasing behavior indicated that repeat customers contributed substantially to overall revenue.
- Revenue distribution varied across regions, identifying high-performing states and cities.
- Seller performance analysis highlighted the strongest revenue-generating sellers and their contribution to total sales.
